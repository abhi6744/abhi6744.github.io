SemanticGuard: A Drift-Aware Selective Re-Embedding and Hybrid Coverage-Gap Ingestion Framework for Efficient RAG Systems

AUTHORS / IN ORDER
  1. Abhinav GLead author
  2. Dr. Vandana Mehndiratta
  3. Dr. Ramesh Chandra Poonia
  4. Dr. Varuna Gupta

Christ University, Bengaluru, India

RAG embeddings quietly decay when the encoder or preprocessing pipeline changes, even when the source text never moves. SemanticGuard ranks documents by semantic drift, re-embeds only the highest-drift items within a fixed budget, and ingests new documents by balancing topic representativeness with coverage-gap novelty. At just 20% repair and 20% ingestion budgets, it preserves most of the retrieval quality of a full rebuild at a fraction of the computation.

01

Drift that can be seen

An embedding-space score that detects stale vectors using cosine disagreement — even when the document text has not changed.

02

Repair within budget

Rank by drift, then re-embed only the highest-drift documents that fit the repair budget. No expensive full-corpus rebuild.

03

Smarter ingestion

New documents are scored by topic representativeness and coverage-gap novelty, with the balance tuned per dataset.

METHOD / THE PIPELINE
  1. 01Encode

    15,000 docs per corpus → 384-d vectors with all-MiniLM-L6-v2.

  2. 02Detect

    Drift = 1 − cosine(stored, fresh). Perturbed vectors ~0.72 vs clean ~0.97 similarity.

  3. 03Repair & ingest

    Re-embed top drift; blend coverage gap + representativeness for arrivals.

  4. 04Evaluate

    P@10, R@10, MRR, nDCG across 5 seeds against Full Rebuild and 7 baselines.

Show full abstract, contributions and keywords

Abstract. Retrieval Augmented Generation systems use vector databases to ground generated answers in retrieved documents. While source text may remain unchanged, changing the embedding model or processing pipeline makes stored embeddings decay. Full corpus re-embedding is resource-heavy. SemanticGuard presents budget-aware document ingestion and selective embedding correction: documents are ranked by semantic drift and the highest-drift items within a budget are re-embedded, while incoming documents are ranked by both topical representativeness and coverage-gap uniqueness. Against Full-Rebuild nDCG@10, SemanticGuard retains 94.9%, 85.7% and 86.1% on arXiv abstracts, Indian news and financial analyst-rated headlines at 20% repair and 20% ingestion budgets, and 95.4%, 85.6% and 89.6% under cross-model drift validation.

Contributions. (1) an embedding-space drift score that detects stale vectors with unchanged text; (2) cost-conscious selective re-embedding with a budget-derived threshold; (3) hybrid document intake blending representativeness and coverage gaps; (4) evaluation on three datasets with real and synthetic drift; (5) computational-cost comparison against partial updates and full rebuilds.

Keywords. Retrieval-Augmented Generation · vector database · semantic drift · selective re-embedding · cost-aware maintenance · coverage-gap ingestion.
